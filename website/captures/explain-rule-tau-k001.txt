TAU-K001: Kernel determinism and purity

Why: Kernel decisions must be deterministic and free of shared mutable state so they can be extracted and proved (Aeneas) and replayed exactly.

Fix: Use `BTreeMap`/`BTreeSet` and plain owned values; pass state explicitly.

Enforced by: Clippy
Applies to tests: no
Exceptions allowed: with #[expect(.., reason)]
  kernel deny
  core -
  edge -
Banned (shared mutability or nondeterministic iteration in the kernel): std::rc::Rc, std::rc::Weak, std::cell::RefCell, std::cell::Cell, std::sync::Arc, std::sync::Mutex, std::sync::RwLock, std::collections::HashMap, std::collections::HashSet
