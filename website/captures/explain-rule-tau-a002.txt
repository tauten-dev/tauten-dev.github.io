TAU-A002: Authority minted outside the edge

Why: Capabilities may only be created at startup in edge crates; minting them deeper defeats the point of passing them.

Fix: Accept the capability as a parameter and create it in the edge crate's `main`.

Enforced by: Clippy
Applies to tests: yes
Exceptions allowed: with #[expect(.., reason)]
  kernel deny
  core deny
  edge -
Banned (mints authority; only an edge crate may): tauten::cap::Authority::acquire
