TAU-N001: Checked arithmetic

Why: Integer overflow and division by zero either panic or wrap silently depending on build profile; both are logic errors agents rarely test.

Fix: Use `checked_*` and return an error on None, or `saturating_*`/`wrapping_*` when that behaviour is the specification. Say which in a comment.

Enforced by: Clippy
Applies to tests: no
Exceptions allowed: with #[expect(.., reason)]
  kernel deny
  core deny
  edge warn
Lints: clippy::arithmetic_side_effects, clippy::integer_division
