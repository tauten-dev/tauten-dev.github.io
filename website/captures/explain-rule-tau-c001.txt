TAU-C001: Structured concurrency only

Why: Detached threads and tasks can outlive their caller, leak errors and race with shutdown.

Fix: Use `std::thread::scope`, or `tokio::task::JoinSet` owned by the caller and awaited before returning.

Enforced by: Clippy
Applies to tests: no
Exceptions allowed: with #[expect(.., reason)]
  kernel deny
  core deny
  edge -
Banned (detached thread or task): std::thread::spawn, std::thread::Builder::spawn, tokio::spawn, tokio::task::spawn, tokio::task::spawn_blocking, tokio::task::spawn_local, async_std::task::spawn
