TAU-L001: No unbounded loops outside the edge

Why: A loop with no exit cannot be reasoned about or terminated by the caller.

Fix: Bound the loop, return a value from it, or move the event loop to the edge.

Enforced by: Clippy
Applies to tests: no
Exceptions allowed: with #[expect(.., reason)]
  kernel deny
  core deny
  edge -
