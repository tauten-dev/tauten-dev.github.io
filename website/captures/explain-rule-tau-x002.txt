TAU-X002: No crate-wide exceptions to profile lints

Why: A crate-level `#![expect(...)]` or `#![allow(...)]` of a profile lint switches the rule off for the whole crate.

Fix: Move the exception to the single item that needs it, with a reason.

Decided by: Tauten
Decides: No crate-level `#![allow]`/`#![expect]` of a profile lint.
Sees: source; complete: yes
Does not see: Attributes and items produced by macros. Read in `src/`, `tests/`, `benches/`, `examples/` and the build script, including inside `cfg_attr`; a lint group counts as every profile rule it may contain (an over-approximation).
Applies to tests: yes
Exceptions allowed: with #[expect(.., reason)]
  kernel deny
  core deny
  edge deny
