TAU-M001: No wildcard arms over enums you own

Why: A `_` arm silently absorbs variants added later; exhaustive matches turn a new variant into a compile error at every decision site.

Fix: List every variant. Group them with `A | B => ...` if they share behaviour.

Enforced by: Clippy
Applies to tests: no
Exceptions allowed: with #[expect(.., reason)]
  kernel deny
  core deny
  edge warn
Lints: clippy::wildcard_enum_match_arm, clippy::match_wildcard_for_single_variants
