TAU-S002: Suspicious placeholder comments

Why: Comments such as 'for now', 'placeholder', 'TODO' or 'FIXME' usually mark unfinished work that tests do not cover.

Fix: Finish the work, or track it in your issue tracker and remove the comment.

Enforced by: Tauten
Applies to tests: yes
Exceptions allowed: with #[expect(.., reason)]
