TAU-S002: Suspicious placeholder comments

Why: Comments such as 'for now', 'placeholder', 'TODO' or 'FIXME' usually mark unfinished work that tests do not cover.

Fix: Finish the work, or track it in your issue tracker and remove the comment.

Decided by: Tauten
Decides: Comments that read like placeholders ("for now", "TODO") are flagged (a text heuristic).
Sees: source; complete: no (a heuristic: it never blocks)
Does not see: Attributes and items produced by macros; files outside a member's `src/`. Placeholder wording it has no pattern for.
Applies to tests: yes
Exceptions allowed: with #[expect(.., reason)]
  kernel warn
  core warn
  edge warn
