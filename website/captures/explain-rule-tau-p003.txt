TAU-P003: No panicking index or slice

Why: `v[i]` and `&s[a..b]` panic on out-of-range or non-char-boundary input; off-by-one errors become crashes.

Fix: Use `.get(i)` / `.get(a..b)` and handle None, iterate instead of indexing, or use `split_at_checked`.

Enforced by: Clippy
Applies to tests: no
Exceptions allowed: with #[expect(.., reason)]
  kernel deny
  core deny
  edge warn
Lints: clippy::indexing_slicing, clippy::string_slice
