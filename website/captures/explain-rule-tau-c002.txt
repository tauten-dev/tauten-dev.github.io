TAU-C002: Async hazards

Why: Holding a lock across `.await` deadlocks, and dropping a future means the work never runs.

Fix: Release guards before awaiting; await or explicitly spawn-and-join futures.

Enforced by: Clippy
Applies to tests: yes
Exceptions allowed: with #[expect(.., reason)]
  kernel deny
  core deny
  edge deny
Lints: clippy::await_holding_lock, clippy::await_holding_refcell_ref, clippy::let_underscore_future, clippy::large_futures
