Proofs

Proofs over the shipped Rust

●Exercised (local and GitHub CI)

"Proved" means: a Lean theorem about the Charon/Aeneas translation of the exact crate that ships, passing the five steps below. tauten's own build decisions (tier rules, severity, exceptions, the baseline ratchet, rule-table validation) live in the no_std crate tauten-check-kernel and are proved this way; cargo tauten init --proofs sets up the same gate for your kernel crates.

The gate

  1. 1 · extractCharon and Aeneas translate the kernel crate to Lean.
  2. 2 · driftThe extraction must match the committed proofs/Extracted/.
  3. 3 · buildlake build checks every proof.
  4. 4 · auditAudit.lean: axioms only propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound; every extracted function covered by a claim.
  5. 5 · mutantsEach deliberately broken copy of the Rust must fail its named theorems.

Not proved, and trusted

Source scanning, diagnostic mapping, the rustc wrapper, workspace loading and the CLI are tested, not proved; so is the authority check (TAU-A002). Charon, Aeneas, Lean and the steps around them are trusted: proofs/TRUST.md.

The kernel's arithmetic and indexing rules duplicate what an Aeneas proof already establishes (dry-run report). P5 subsumes them only for functions with a recorded, current, total proof. Writfold's adoption branch subsumes 142 of its 159 extracted-kernel findings this way, after rewriting four helpers to be total (get instead of indexing; 10 more findings disappeared with the indexing). The remaining 7 are in splice, whose only panic is a length overflow past usize::MAX. (docs/STATUS.md, Known gaps)
