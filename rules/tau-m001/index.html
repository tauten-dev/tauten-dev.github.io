Rules / TAU-M001

No wildcard arms over enums you own

enforced by Clippy kernel deny core deny edge warn
Applies to tests: no Exceptions: #[expect] + reason

Why

A _ arm silently absorbs variants added later; exhaustive matches turn a new variant into a compile error at every decision site.

Fix

List every variant. Group them with A | B => ... if they share behaviour.

What it checks

Lints: clippy::wildcard_enum_match_arm, clippy::match_wildcard_for_single_variants
