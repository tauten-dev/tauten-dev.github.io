Rules / TAU-T001

Every workspace crate declares a tier

decided by Tauten kernel deny core deny edge deny
Applies to tests: yes Exceptions: never

Why

Rules depend on the tier; an undeclared crate would escape the profile.

Fix

Add [package.metadata.tauten] with tier = "kernel", "core" or "edge" to the crate's Cargo.toml.

Seen in fixtures

In the terminal
