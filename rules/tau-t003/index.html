Rules / TAU-T003

Kernel dependencies are allowlisted

enforced by Tauten kernel deny core – edge –
Applies to tests: yes Exceptions: never

Why

Each external dependency of the kernel widens what must be trusted and may block proof extraction.

Fix

Remove the dependency, or list it in [package.metadata.tauten] kernel-deps = ["name"] with a justification in review.
