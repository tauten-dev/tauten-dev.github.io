Rules / TAU-S002

Suspicious placeholder comments

decided by Tauten kernel warn core warn edge warn
Applies to tests: yes Exceptions: never

Why

Comments such as 'for now', 'placeholder', 'TODO' or 'FIXME' usually mark unfinished work that tests do not cover.

Fix

Finish the work, or track it in your issue tracker and remove the comment.
