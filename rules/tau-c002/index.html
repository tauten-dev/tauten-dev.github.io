Rules / TAU-C002

Async hazards

decided by Clippy kernel deny core deny edge deny
Applies to tests: yes Exceptions: #[expect] + reason

Why

Holding a lock across .await deadlocks, and dropping a future means the work never runs.

Fix

Release guards before awaiting; await or explicitly spawn-and-join futures.

What it checks

Lints: clippy::await_holding_lock, clippy::await_holding_refcell_ref, clippy::let_underscore_future, clippy::large_futures
