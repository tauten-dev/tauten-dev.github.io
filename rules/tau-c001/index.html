Rules / TAU-C001

Structured concurrency only

enforced by Clippy kernel deny core deny edge –
Applies to tests: no Exceptions: #[expect] + reason

Why

Detached threads and tasks can outlive their caller, leak errors and race with shutdown.

Fix

Use std::thread::scope, or tokio::task::JoinSet owned by the caller and awaited before returning.

What it checks

Banned in kernel, core (detached thread or task), 7 methods: std::thread::spawn, std::thread::Builder::spawn, tokio::spawn, tokio::task::spawn, tokio::task::spawn_blocking, tokio::task::spawn_local, async_std::task::spawn
