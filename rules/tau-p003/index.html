Rules / TAU-P003

No panicking index or slice

enforced by Clippy kernel deny core deny edge warn
Applies to tests: no Exceptions: #[expect] + reason

Why

v[i] and &s[a..b] panic on out-of-range or non-char-boundary input; off-by-one errors become crashes.

Fix

Use .get(i) / .get(a..b) and handle None, iterate instead of indexing, or use split_at_checked.

What it checks

Lints: clippy::indexing_slicing, clippy::string_slice
