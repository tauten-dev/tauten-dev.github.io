Rules / TAU-K001

Kernel determinism and purity

decided by Clippy kernel deny core – edge –
Applies to tests: no Exceptions: #[expect] + reason

Why

Kernel decisions must be deterministic and free of shared mutable state so they can be extracted and proved (Aeneas) and replayed exactly.

Fix

Use BTreeMap/BTreeSet and plain owned values; pass state explicitly.

What it checks

Banned in kernel (shared mutability or nondeterministic iteration in the kernel), 9 types: std::rc::Rc, std::rc::Weak, std::cell::RefCell, std::cell::Cell, std::sync::Arc, std::sync::Mutex, std::sync::RwLock, std::collections::HashMap, std::collections::HashSet
